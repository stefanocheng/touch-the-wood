Touch the Wood

Knock on wood, wherever you are. An iPhone app.

Tap the wooden surface and you get a real knock: a sharp rap you feel through the phone, a wooden sound, a ripple spreading from your fingertip, and a word of reassurance. It keeps a running count of your knocks and a daily streak.

Support

Questions, bugs, or suggestions go to the issue tracker. Opening an issue needs a free GitHub account.

Privacy

The app collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. Your count and streak stay on your device. Full details in the privacy policy.

Requirements

  • iPhone running iOS 16 or later
  • Haptics need a device with a Taptic Engine; other devices fall back to a standard vibration
Touch the Wood is an independent app. Source code is in this repository.

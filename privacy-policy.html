Privacy Policy

Touch the Wood for iPhone · Effective 13 September 2026

Touch the Wood does not collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no network connection of any kind.

This policy explains what Touch the Wood does and does not do with information. It is short because the app does very little.

Information the app does not collect

Touch the Wood collects no personal information whatsoever. Specifically, it does not collect or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact details
  • Your location
  • Your device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or IP address
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or behavioural data
  • Contacts, photos, calendars, health data, or any other data from your device

The app contains no analytics frameworks, no advertising frameworks, and no third-party software development kits of any kind.

Information stored on your device

Touch the Wood saves a small amount of information locally on your iPhone so the app can work as expected:

  • The number of times you have tapped the wood
  • Your current daily streak and the date of your most recent tap
  • Your preferences for the knock sound and haptics

This information stays in the app's own storage on your device. It is never transmitted anywhere, it is not backed up to any service operated by the developer, and the developer cannot see it. Deleting the app removes it permanently. You can also clear the count and streak at any time from the app's settings screen.

Network access

Touch the Wood makes no network requests. The app functions identically with the device in Airplane Mode. No servers are operated in connection with this app.

Sharing

The app includes a share button that opens the standard iOS share sheet with a short line of text. What happens to that text is determined entirely by you and by whichever app you choose to send it to. Touch the Wood does not see, record, or transmit anything you share.

Children

Touch the Wood is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13. Because no information is collected from any user, there is nothing that could be collected from a child.

Your rights

Regulations such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, and delete personal data held about you. Touch the Wood holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. The information described above never leaves your device and is under your control at all times.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what information it handles, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date at the top of this page will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be raised at github.com/stefanocheng/touch-the-wood/issues.

Touch the Wood is an independent app. This policy covers the Touch the Wood iPhone app and nothing else.
